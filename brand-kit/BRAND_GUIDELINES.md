# VitalSync Brand Guidelines & Assets

## 1. Brand Identity
- **Name:** `VitalSync`
- **Tagline (EN):** `Your Comprehensive Health Sync · Health. Movement. Life.`
- **Tagline (SK):** `Tvoja Komplexná Zdravotná Synchronizácia · Zdravie. Pohyb. Život.`

## 2. Color Palette
| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Brand Deep Navy | `#0A365C` | Primary logotype (`Vital`), deep badge contrast |
| Brand Teal-Cyan | `#1E88A8` | Secondary accent, water wave midtone |
| Brand Turquoise | `#34C6C3` | Logotype (`Sync`), active telemetry & highlights |
| Pure White | `#FFFFFF` / `#FDFFFF` | Light splash & icon surface |
| Charcoal Slate | `#243030` | Dark feature graphic & dark theme foundation |

## 3. Typography
- **Display & Wordmark:** `Montserrat` (`R.font.montserrat`) — Light, Regular, Bold (`Vital` Bold Navy + `Sync` Bold Turquoise)
- **UI & Multilingual Body (EN/SK/CZ/HU/PL):** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`R.font.plus_jakarta_sans`)
- **Biometric Numerals:** `JetBrains Mono` (`R.font.jetbrains_mono`)
